Forecast New York City's Electricity Demand for the Next 7 Days using XGBoost!

This application uses machine learning to predict NYC's electrical grid load based on live weather forecasts and historical patterns.

20 years of hourly data was downloaded from NYISO (New York Independent System Operator) to train and validate the model.

Sliding-window cross validation was used to compute an accuracy of 97.1%, using the Mean Absolute Percentage Error Error across 52 models: 1 per week for a year.

Predictions are heavily reliant on weather data: The 7 day temperature forecast is imported in real-time from the National Weather Service API.
